---
title: "Drawer"
description: "A React drawer that slides in from any edge with a Liquid Glass surface and swipe dismissal."
---

`Drawer` is an integrated wrapper around Base UI's `Drawer`. It renders the portal, backdrop, edge-aligned popup, title, description, optional body, and a custom action area internally, with swipe dismissal and optional snap points.

Unlike the centered `Dialog`, `Drawer` slides in from an edge of the screen, which makes it a good fit for mobile settings panels, filters, and side navigation.

## Basic example

The drawer opens from the bottom by default. `open` controls visibility and `footer` holds your own buttons; buttons do not close the drawer automatically, so update `open` yourself as the example does.

<Example name="drawer/basic" />

## Side panels

`direction` reuses Base UI's `swipeDirection` values: `down` slides in from the bottom, `up` from the top, and `left` and `right` from the matching side. It defaults to `down`, and the component adjusts the popup position, corner radii, enter animation, and swipe dismissal direction together.

<Example name="drawer/sides" />

## Gesture dismissal

On touch screens, drag the drawer in the direction set by `direction` to dismiss it. On desktop, the backdrop, Escape key, and action buttons remain available.

<Example name="drawer/gesture" />

## Snap points

`snapPoints`, `defaultSnapPoint`, `snapPoint`, `onSnapPointChange`, and `snapToSequentialPoints` pass through to Base UI and can make a bottom or top drawer settle between preset heights.

<Example name="drawer/snap-points" />

## Backdrop dismissal

`disablePointerDismissal` prevents the backdrop from closing the drawer while Escape and the action buttons still work. Use it for flows that may contain unsaved work.

`Drawer` also forwards Base UI `Drawer.Root` props such as `actionsRef`, `handle`, `triggerId`, `defaultTriggerId`, `snapPoints`, `defaultSnapPoint`, `snapPoint`, `onSnapPointChange`, and `snapToSequentialPoints`.

`style` is intentionally not supported: the glass theme owns the backdrop, popup, and content. Use `className` for popup layout. Drawer styling is internal and is not customizable per instance; tune it through the shared `--ui-glass-*` tokens instead.

Base UI supplies the `dialog` role, `aria-labelledby` from the title, `aria-describedby` from the description, focus trapping, scroll locking, focus return, backdrop and Escape dismissal, and swipe gestures.
