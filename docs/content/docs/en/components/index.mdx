`@xingxingmofashu/ui` ships twenty-five accessible components across elements, forms, overlays, and feedback. Every component page includes live examples, API props, and usage notes. Each component exposes `ui` slots keyed by `data-slot` in addition to `className`.

<Callout type="info" title="Live examples">
  Every component page includes live examples, and the marketing home page
  previews a selection of components. Open any page below to try the real
  component in both themes.
</Callout>

## Element

- [Accordion](/docs/en/components/accordion) — Disclosure rows that share one glass surface.
- [Button](/docs/en/components/button) — Rounded glass actions with icon slots and five sizes.
- [Card](/docs/en/components/card) — A composed panel with header, body, and footer.
- [Scroll Area](/docs/en/components/scroll-area) — A glass panel with custom scrollbars.
- [Tabs](/docs/en/components/tabs) — A glass tab list with an animated active indicator.
- [Toggle](/docs/en/components/toggle) — A two-state glass button with a pressed thumb.
- [Toggle Group](/docs/en/components/toggle-group) — Single and multiple selection with a liquid selected state.

## Form

- [Form](/docs/en/components/form) — A native form with consolidated errors and form-level validation.
- [Checkbox](/docs/en/components/checkbox) — A glass tile with an animated check and indeterminate state.
- [Checkbox Group](/docs/en/components/checkbox-group) — Labelled sets of checkboxes with shared state and descriptions.
- [Field](/docs/en/components/field) — A label, description, and validation message around one form control.
- [Fieldset](/docs/en/components/fieldset) — A fieldset with a Liquid Glass legend for related fields.
- [Input](/docs/en/components/input) — A glass text field with icon slots, labeled through Field.
- [Radio Group](/docs/en/components/radio-group) — Circular glass radio controls with labels, descriptions, and keyboard navigation.
- [Select](/docs/en/components/select) — A glass select with labels, descriptions, disabled options, and five sizes.
- [Slider](/docs/en/components/slider) — Single values, ranges, and vertical controls.
- [Switch](/docs/en/components/switch) — A liquid thumb that stretches while it moves.

## Overlay

- [Alert Dialog](/docs/en/components/alert-dialog) — A modal confirmation with built-in cancel and confirm actions.
- [Dialog](/docs/en/components/dialog) — A general-purpose modal with a custom action area.
- [Drawer](/docs/en/components/drawer) — An edge-aligned panel with swipe dismissal.
- [Menu](/docs/en/components/menu) — A glass action menu with groups, separators, icons, and shortcuts.
- [Popover](/docs/en/components/popover) — A non-modal glass panel anchored to its trigger.
- [Toast](/docs/en/components/toast) — Stacked glass notifications with status icons and actions.
- [Tooltip](/docs/en/components/tooltip) — A compact glass hint shown on hover or focus.

## Feedback

- [Progress](/docs/en/components/progress) — A linear glass progress bar with determinate and indeterminate states.
